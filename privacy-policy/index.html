SpendWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

SpendWise is a personal expense tracking application designed to help users record, organize, and analyze their expenses.

Information You Enter

SpendWise allows you to enter expense information such as:

  • Merchant names
  • Expense amounts
  • Expense categories
  • Expense dates
  • Notes associated with expenses

How Your Information Is Stored

Expense information entered into SpendWise is stored locally on your Android device using the application's local database.

SpendWise does not require you to create an account and the current version does not transmit your expense information to a developer-controlled server.

Data Collection and Sharing

The current version of SpendWise does not sell, rent, or share your expense information with third parties.

SpendWise does not use your expense information for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

Third-Party Services

The current version of SpendWise does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Data Security

Expense information is stored locally on your device. The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your Android device, including device access controls such as a screen lock.

Data Retention and Deletion

Expense information remains stored locally on your device until you delete the information through the application or remove the application's locally stored data.

Because the current version of SpendWise does not maintain a developer-controlled server containing your expense information, there is no server-side copy of your expense data maintained by the developer.

Permissions

SpendWise requests only the permissions necessary for the application to function. Expense information is stored locally on the user's device and is not uploaded to a developer-controlled server.

Children's Privacy

SpendWise is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SpendWise gains new features or changes how information is handled. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
